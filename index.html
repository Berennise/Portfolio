✦ Map of the work

Twelve disciplines around one brand.

In the center, one question: how does this brand feel when you touch it? Each discipline answers from its own place, but all of them share the same rules of color, type and voice.

12 disciplines · 1 system · 1 designer

✦ Origin

How Baza started.

Baza is the client’s brand. My role was to help them find how to communicate it. It began as an idea in 2024 and reached its first products in 2025; before any logo, we researched identity, the market and the social context, and defined which products, at what prices and for whom. That research was done by hand, before AI tools were part of the process.

  1. 2024Research

    Identity, the market and the social context, studied in depth before designing anything.

  2. 2025Identity

    A brand that talks about its public and their culture, with close, warm color.

  3. 2025–26Packaging

    Many rounds with the factories until each box told its product’s story.

  4. AlwaysLearning

    A process of mutual growth: I learned with the client how Mercado Libre works, where they stood in the market and how they wanted to be seen. It is still going.

✦ Roots

Argentine, Latin American and Armenian.

The identity is Argentine and Latin American, and it also carries the Armenian roots of the client. Both cultures met during the exploration: in the food on the table, in the patterns on the boxes and in the details of the design.

  • FoodTraditions from both kitchens on the same table.
  • PatternsTile-like grids and textures drawn for every box.
  • DesignColor and craft from both cultures, never as costume.
